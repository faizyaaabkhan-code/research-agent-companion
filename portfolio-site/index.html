About me

Creative curiosity.
Practical business thinking.

I’m Faizyaab Khan, a BBA graduate with professional experience in e-commerce operations, product listings, catalogue design, website management, buyer inquiries, and on-page SEO.

Alongside that work, I’ve explored AI image and video generation, AI filmmaking, website building, and AI-assisted game development. I’m interested in how emerging creative tools can help brands communicate product value more effectively.

My approach is hands-on: learn the brief, plan the output, test the workflow, and present the result clearly. I’m continuing to develop conventional editing and automation skills, and I’m careful to define what each project can realistically deliver.
